From Synthesis to Delivery

Our quality process

A rigorous four-stage pipeline ensures every vial meets the highest standards of purity and documentation before leaving our facility.

01

Solid-Phase Synthesis

Peptides are assembled residue-by-residue using automated Fmoc-SPPS on validated resin platforms, with in-process coupling efficiency monitoring throughout.

02

Purification

Crude product undergoes preparative reverse-phase HPLC, removing truncated sequences, deletion analogues, and synthesis by-products to achieve >98–99% purity.

03

Analytical Verification

Each batch undergoes analytical HPLC re-injection and ESI-MS confirmation. Identity, purity, and molecular weight are verified against specification and fully recorded.

04

Lyophilisation & Dispatch

Verified material is lyophilised, filled under inert atmosphere, sealed with full traceability data, and dispatched same-day (orders before 12 PM EST, Mon–Fri). We ship internationally via DHL and domestically within Switzerland via DPD Switzerland.
